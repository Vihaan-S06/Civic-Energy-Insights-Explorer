## OBJECTIVE

Right now users can:

- Create teams
- Join teams
- Make pledges
- Earn points
- View a leaderboard

This is good, but it lacks purpose.

The goal is to make users feel like they are participating in an actual community campaign.

Every action should answer:

"Why does this matter?"

# PHASE 10 — IMPACT EXPLAINER

Every estimate should explain itself.

Instead of:

8.4 tons saved

Show:

Your team's completed missions are estimated to reduce emissions by approximately 8.4 metric tons of CO₂ per year.

That's roughly equal to:

🌳 420 trees planted

🚗 Taking 2 gasoline cars off the road for a year

🏠 Powering 5 average homes with clean electricity

Include a tooltip explaining that these are educational estimates based on EPA conversion factors and average household energy use.
