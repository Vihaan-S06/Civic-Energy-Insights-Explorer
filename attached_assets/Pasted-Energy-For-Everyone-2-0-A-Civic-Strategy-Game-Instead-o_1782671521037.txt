Energy For Everyone 2.0
A Civic Strategy Game
Instead of just reading statistics, users become energy planners.

They're responsible for designing an electric grid while balancing four competing goals:

Keep electricity affordable

Keep emissions low

Keep the lights on

Keep people happy

There is no perfect solution, which makes the game interesting.

The goal is to teach users why outcomes changed, not just show new numbers.

Design philosophy
The experience should never feel like a collection of disconnected pages. Everything should revolve around a single gameplay loop:

Learn → Make a decision → See realistic consequences → Adapt → Improve.

That approach makes the project educational, replayable, and engaging while staying grounded in real energy-system tradeoffs rather than turning it into a simple point-collecting game.
