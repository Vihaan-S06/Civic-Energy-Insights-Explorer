Given where you are, I would not add more pages. You already have a strong MVP. The next additions should increase credibility, usefulness, and demo impact.

Tier 1 — Highest Impact
1. Personal Energy Impact Calculator
Add a new section to Take Action:

Users enter:

Home size

State/region

Monthly electricity bill

EV ownership (yes/no)

Output:

Estimated annual electricity usage

Estimated CO₂ footprint

How much switching to a greener grid would reduce emissions

This makes the project personal instead of just informational.

2. Real EIA Data Integration
This is still the biggest missing piece.

When someone asks:

"Where does this data come from?"

You want to answer:

"Directly from the U.S. Energy Information Administration."

That instantly boosts credibility.

3. Region Comparison Mode
Add:

Compare Regions

Example:

California vs Texas

ERCOT vs PJM

Show:

Renewable %

CO₂ intensity

Price

Grid Health

This creates interesting discoveries and is great for demos.

4. "Why Is My Grid Like This?"
AI-style explanation engine.

Example:

Texas has lower prices because natural gas provides a large share of generation. However, this increases carbon intensity compared to regions with larger renewable portfolios.

Generate a plain-English explanation for every region.

5. Historical Milestones Timeline
New card on Trends:

Largest renewable growth year

Lowest carbon year

Highest electricity price year

Biggest change in generation mix

Makes the data tell a story.

6. Grid Health Forecast
Simple prediction model:

Based on recent trends, renewable share is projected to increase from 31% to 34% over the next 12 months.

Doesn't need AI—just trend extrapolation.
